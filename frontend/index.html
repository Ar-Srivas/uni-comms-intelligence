About this tool

University Communications Intelligence automatically analyzes unstructured institutional notices, policy updates, and academic syllabi.

It accurately determines notice categories, identifies primary stakeholder audiences, extracts critical deadlines and contact information, and generates tailored email drafts for students and faculty.

Example input

Sample updated syllabus & midterm notice:

DEPARTMENT OF COMPUTER SCIENCE & ENGINEERING

How the tool works

  1. Stage 1: Classification

    TF-IDF + Linear Classifier classifies the notice category (e.g., examination, academic) and predicts target audiences (students, faculty, administrators).

  2. Stage 2: Entity Extraction

    Transformer-based Named Entity Recognition (NER) and regex extract dates, deadlines, emails, organizations, and institutional roles.

  3. Stage 3: Persona Summarization

    A fine-tuned sequence-to-sequence model synthesizes audience-specific email drafts targeting student actions and faculty responsibilities.
