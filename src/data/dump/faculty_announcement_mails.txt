Subject: Call for Faculty Mentors: IUCEE Student Chapter Innovation and Sustainability Competition

Content: Dear Faculty Members,

Greetings!

The IUCEE Student Chapter is inviting project proposals for its innovation and sustainability competition. The initiative aims to encourage interdisciplinary collaboration and support student teams working on practical solutions aligned with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).

Faculty members are encouraged to mentor interested student teams and guide them in developing clear problem statements, feasible solution approaches, and well-structured project proposals. Teams may include students from different engineering disciplines, and interdisciplinary participation is particularly encouraged. Proposals that demonstrate a strong understanding of the problem and include a working proof of concept may receive preference during shortlisting.

Key points for faculty mentors:

• Each team may include up to five students and one faculty mentor.
• Teams should satisfy the year-of-study and interdisciplinary requirements specified in the competition circular.
• Proposed projects should address one or more of the eligible SDG targets listed in the attached annexure.
• The proposal should describe the problem, proposed solution, implementation approach, expected impact, and feasibility of developing a demonstrable prototype.
• The final submission deadline and selection timeline are provided in the official circular.

Faculty members who wish to mentor a team are requested to discuss the scope of the project with the students early and ensure that the proposal is reviewed before submission. Please direct students to the official participation form and attached guidelines for complete details.

We look forward to your support in nurturing innovative, responsible, and impactful student projects.
