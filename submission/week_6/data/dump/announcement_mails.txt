IUCEE Student Chapter Competition 2025

IUCEE is thrilled to announce an exciting competition designed to foster innovation, collaboration, and impactful contributions from its dynamic network of student chapters. This competition serves as a platform for you to showcase your chapter's activities, projects, and initiatives that align with IUCEE's mission of enhancing the quality and relevance of engineering education.

We encourage all IUCEE student chapters to participate in this competition. This competition will not only provide recognition for your efforts but also serve as a valuable platform for sharing best practices and inspiring other chapters within the IUCEE network.

The competition is open to all active student members of IUCEE-affiliated engineering college chapters.

What is the Competition?

Competition involves a project proposal from each team based on Sustainable Design Goals and prioritize environmental sustainability, resource efficiency, and the use of eco-friendly materials and practices. List of SDG goals selected for the competition are attached as an Annexure 1. Selected projects will be considered for partial financial support by IUCEE Foundation to build working prototypes which needs to be demonstrated at IASF in January 2026.

Participation Guidelines

Student teams should consist of a max 5 students and a faculty mentor. Out of the team of 5, at least 3 students Contatshould be from 2nd and/or 3rd year of engineering. Students should be from different departments for interdisciplinary categories. At least 2 of the participants should have undergone a course on Design Thinking.

Next Step: Submit detailed project proposal (preferably with Proof of Concept) by June 10th 2025

Participating teams will work on any of the targets under SDGs in Annexure 1, and come up with a) problem definition and b) solution which can help in achieving that target.

The teams are expected to submit a detailed project proposal with problem definition, proposed solution and details of how the solution works. The teams who submit Proof of Concept along with the project proposal would be given preference in selection.

IUCEE will shortlist the projects based on their alignment to SDG, innovative solution and potential to succeed as a product in the long term. The list of selected projects for further work will be communicated by July 1, 2025. IUCEE will provide mentorship to shortlisted projects to develop their idea further and convert them into a demonstrable prototype by Dec 2025.

Top 5 shortlisted project teams will be invited to bring their prototype and demonstrate it at ICTIEE 2026 IASF. Panel of judges will choose a winner of the competition during IASF & will receive suitable recognition & award.

IUCEE can facilitate the winners to incubate it as a business idea if they choose to pursue it further.
